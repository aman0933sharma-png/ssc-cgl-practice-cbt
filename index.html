General Instructions — CGL Tier-I Practice

  1. Total duration is 60 minutes. Each of the four parts is locked to 15 minutes.
  2. 100 questions: Reasoning 25, GA 25, Quant 25, English 25.
  3. Correct = +2. Wrong = −0.50. Unattempted = 0.
  4. A new random paper is generated every time you start.
  5. Select an option with the radio button, then press Save and Next.
  6. Mark for Review parks a question inside the same 15-minute section.
  7. When a section timer hits 00:00 you move to the next part automatically.
